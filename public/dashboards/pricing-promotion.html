Pricing & promotion

Najma Foods raised poultry and dairy prices 6% in March. Its main competitor did not follow, and the trade marketing team keeps asking for deeper discounts. This dashboard uses two years of weekly sales to measure how customers react to price, then lets the team test a plan before committing budget.

The business question

What happens to volume and profit if we change price or run a promotion, and how do we compare with competitors?
