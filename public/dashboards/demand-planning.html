Demand, stock and supply

Najma Foods plans 20 frozen and chilled products monthly. The planning team produces a statistical forecast, then sales adjusts it in the monthly S&OP meeting. Some products keep running out while chilled stock expires in the warehouse, and on-time delivery to customers is slipping.

The business question

How good is our forecast, which products will run out or expire next, and where is the supply chain letting customers down?
